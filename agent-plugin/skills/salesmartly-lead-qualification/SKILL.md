# Lead qualification

Qualify the opportunity conversationally, not as an interrogation.

- Confirm the product category and use case.
- Ask for estimated quantity or order cadence.
- Confirm destination market and desired timing.
- Ask about customization or compliance needs only when relevant.
- Do not claim the customer is qualified until enough evidence exists.
- Do not create a contact, consent record, budget, or purchase authority that the customer did not provide.
- This skill handles inbound messages only. Any proactive follow-up remains a draft until a human approves it.

Recommend one stage: `new`, `qualified`, or `quote`.
